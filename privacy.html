Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Cascade Piano doesn't track you, show ads, or sell your information. Your songs, recordings, projects and videos stay on your own computer.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. Like any website, the company that hosts it (GitHub) receives basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When you click Download, the installer is downloaded from GitHub, and the page asks GitHub which version is the newest.

Buying a licence

Payments are handled by Polar, who act as our reseller (the "merchant of record"). Polar collects your name, email address, payment details and country to process the order, calculate tax and send your licence key. We never see your full card details. Polar's own privacy policy applies to that information.

We can see your name, email address and order details in our Polar account so that we can help you with your order, refunds and support.

The app

The app works offline. It only uses the internet for these things:

  • Unlocking and checking your licence. When you enter a licence key, the app sends the key and your computer's name to Polar, so you can see which computers use your key. About once a week afterwards, it checks with Polar that the key is still valid.
  • Updates. The app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads them.
  • Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

The app doesn't send us your songs, files, recordings, practice scores or how you use it.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you. We don't send marketing emails.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@cascadepiano.com. We may need to keep order records for tax reasons.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@cascadepiano.com.
